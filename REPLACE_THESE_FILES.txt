PICKUPPASS PHASE 3 — CUMULATIVE UPDATE 9
==========================================

This is a CUMULATIVE replacement pack. It contains the PickupPass replacement files from the earlier Phase 1/2/3 updates plus Phase 3 Update 9.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Stop the backend and close/stop the Android app build if running.
2. Extract the protected archive.
3. Open the PickupPass_Phase3_Cumulative_Update_9 folder.
4. Copy everything inside it into the ROOT of your existing "PickupPass - Copy" project.
5. Choose Replace / Overwrite when Windows asks.
6. Do not delete project files that are not present in this replacement pack.
7. Run your normal backend tests and Android build before production deployment.

UPDATE 9 — SAAS OPERATIONS HEALTH + RECONCILIATION DASHBOARD
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Adds a Master Admin Operations Health dashboard.
- Tenant health states: Healthy / Attention Needed / Billing Risk / Over Quota / Suspended.
- Materializes deduplicated operational alerts instead of recalculating all rules on every screen open.
- Immediate GCash payment-review alert when a school submits a manual GCash payment notice.
- GCash confirmation/rejection resolves/recomputes the affected tenant.
- Overdue and due-soon invoice alerts.
- Trial, subscription-period, and grace-period expiry alerts.
- Near-quota warning (default 90%) and over-quota detection for students/staff/campuses.
- Billing invoice/receipt/reminder email failure tracking with automatic alert resolution after a successful retry.
- Tenant-scoped health refresh after billing/subscription/status/quota mutations.
- Hourly subscription lifecycle and invoice-overdue transitions refresh affected tenant health immediately.
- Daily full platform health reconciliation by default, stale-view lazy refresh, plus a manual Refresh Operations action.
- Operational alerts remain outside the student QR verification/release transaction path.
