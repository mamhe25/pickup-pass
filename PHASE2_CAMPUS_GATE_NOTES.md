# Phase 2 Update 10 — Campus & Pickup Gate Management

Adds tenant-scoped `campuses` and `pickupGates` records plus a School Admin screen for creating, archiving and reactivating them. Archiving a campus disables its gates. Existing schools remain single-site unless they choose to configure campuses. No pickup queue or required parent check-in is introduced.

This update establishes the physical-location layer used by the next scanner/reporting enhancement, where staff can tag each release with the pickup gate used.
