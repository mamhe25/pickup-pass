The pickup workflow

Four clear steps from arrival to release.

A simple operational sequence helps staff move quickly without removing the identity check that matters most.

01

Present the pass

The authorized guardian opens the latest valid pickup pass for the student.

02

Scan at the gate

Staff scan the credential and PickupPass checks the active pickup request context.

03

Verify the guardian

The staff member visually confirms the person present against the authorized guardian identity.

04

Approve and record

The student is released only after approval, and the completed handoff becomes part of the dismissal history.

Questions

What schools usually ask first.

The essentials about devices, guardians, school operations, and getting started.

How does the pickup pass stay secure?

Pickup passes are time-limited and generated for the student pickup workflow. Staff still verify the authorized guardian before approving release.

What devices does a school need?

Parents and staff can use the PickupPass Android experience, while administrative workflows are available through the web portal. Staff scanning uses the device camera rather than dedicated scanner hardware.

Can another family member pick up a student?

PickupPass supports authorized guardian relationships so families can manage who is permitted to participate in the pickup process.

Can PickupPass support multiple schools?

The platform includes school-scoped administration and platform-level management designed around multiple school tenants.

What does implementation look like?

A rollout centers on school setup, staff access, student and guardian data, pickup configuration, and testing the complete dismissal flow before launch.
