Why PickupPass

Manual dismissal is a safety gap hiding in plain sight.

Paper sign-out sheets, shouted names, and handwritten notes make it easy for the wrong adult to walk away with a child — and impossible to prove who authorized what. PickupPass turns dismissal into a verified, logged, real-time workflow that parents, teachers, and administrators can all trust.

Questions

Frequently asked

How does the QR pickup pass stay secure?

Each pass is tied to a specific guardian and child, and it refreshes on a short timer. A screenshot taken earlier won't verify, so passes can't be forwarded or reused.

What devices do we need?

Parents and teachers use the PickupPass Android app; administrators use the web portal in any modern browser. No special hardware — a phone camera handles scanning.

Can other family members pick up a child?

Yes. Parents can add trusted backup guardians, each with their own verified pass, and revoke access at any time in one tap.

Does it work for more than one school?

PickupPass supports multiple schools on one platform, each with its own students, staff, and branding, managed independently.
