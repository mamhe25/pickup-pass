Branding guidelines

A simple school mark remains clear on phones, scanners and parent screens.

1Use the official school markAvoid campaign graphics, announcements, slogans or temporary event artwork.
2Square artwork works bestUse comfortable padding around seals or symbols so the mark is not clipped in compact UI.
3Transparent backgrounds are preferredThey adapt cleanly to light and dark PickupPass surfaces.
4Keep files lightweightPickupPass accepts PNG, JPEG and WebP images up to 2 MB.
After upload, the shared school-branding cache is cleared so the new logo appears in navigation on the next page load instead of waiting for the normal cache lifetime.
